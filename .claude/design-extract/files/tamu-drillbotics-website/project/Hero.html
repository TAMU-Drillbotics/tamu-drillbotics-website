§ 02 Mission OUR VALUES
What we are building toward

A program, not a club.

A year-long engineering effort, three commitments. These shape every decision we make on the rig and in the lab.

  1. 01 EDUCATION

    Build full-stack engineers.

    Students who understand mechanical design, embedded systems, edge computing, and machine learning not as separate disciplines, but as complementing fields that the industry increasingly demands together.

    Disciplines MECH EMBEDDED EDGE ML
  2. 02 INDUSTRY

    Bring autonomy to oil & gas.

    The industry runs on some of the most complex and dangerous operations on earth. We’re building the next generation of engineers who automate them.

    Domain UPSTREAM DRILLING SAFETY-CRITICAL
  3. 03 ENGINEERING

    Start small, think at scale.

    Our rig is scaled-down, but the control systems, sensor fusion, and ML pipelines we develop mirror what’s being deployed in the field today.

    Stack CONTROL SENSOR FUSION ML PIPELINES
